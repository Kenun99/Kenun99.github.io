Biography

Weimin Chen is a PhD Candidate in Department of Computing at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, under the supervision of Prof. Xiapu Luo. Before that, he received his B.S. in Cyber Security from BUPT in 2021, supervised by Prof. Haoyu Wang.

His research interests span blockchain security, especially smart contract, binary translation, fuzzing, and CUDA.
